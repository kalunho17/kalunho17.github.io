About the channel

This channel explains AI the way a board paper would.

Sourced, structured and short. Every episode ends with a decision you can make and a document you can use.

Who it is for

It is made for the people who decide.

You run a business, a function or a team, and AI is on your agenda. You do not need a technical background. You need the numbers, the options and a clear view of what they mean for your P&L.

What you get

Every episode ends with something you can use.

  • The numbers. Sourced figures and charts redrawn from the original data.
  • The decision. What the evidence means for your P&L, and what to decide.
  • The handout. A free PDF with every chart and every source, linked in the video description.
  • The deck. The editable PowerPoint version, for newsletter subscribers.

Editorial rules

Five rules apply to every episode.

  • Research first. Every episode starts with the research, not with an opinion.
  • Cited like a paper. Every figure is numbered [1], [2] and listed in a full reference list.
  • Redrawn, never pasted. Charts are rebuilt from the source data, not copied from a report.
  • Nothing invented. Illustrative examples are labelled as illustrative. Everything else has a source.
  • A decision and a document. Every episode ends with a decision you can make and a document you can take into your next meeting.

What the channel avoids

Some topics stay off the channel.

  • Hype. If the evidence is thin, the episode says so.
  • Investment advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell a security.
  • Confidential material. No confidential employer or client information appears on the channel.
  • Speculation about people. Named individuals are not the subject of guesses about their motives or plans.
