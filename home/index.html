The questions

Why, not only what.

The videos explain the forces behind what is happening: cost curves, compute, chips, energy, capital, supply chains. Right now mostly AI, because that is where the movement is.

  • Why did the price of a model's answer fall so fast?
  • Why are chips, not software, the bottleneck?
  • Why has electricity become a technology question?
  • Why do some companies run their AI in-house, and others not?

What comes with each video.

Made for people who have to understand a technology shift well enough to explain it to others at work: managers, engineers outside the specialty, and anyone who presents.

  • The numbers

    Figures from the original sources, with the citations.

  • The explanation

    The technical part, in plain terms.

  • The handout

    A free PDF with the charts and sources, linked in the description and on the videos page. Subscribers get the editable deck.

Behind the videos

How the videos are made.

Each video starts with the research. Figures are cited, charts are redrawn from the source data, and illustrative examples are labelled as such. Where no source has the number, I measure it myself and publish the experiment.

I lead the AI Lab of a large German company and hold a doctorate in computer vision. The videos are a personal project: my way of explaining, outside work, what I see happening in technology.
