5. YouTube videos (loaded only after a click)

The home page shows a preview of the latest video. The preview is part of this website. No data is sent to YouTube while you only look at it.

When you click play, the YouTube player loads from youtube-nocookie.com, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. From that moment, YouTube receives data such as your IP address and the page you are on. YouTube may store cookies or similar information on your device and may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. If you are signed in to a Google account, YouTube can link the visit to your account.

Legal basis: your consent, given by clicking play (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR and § 25 (1) TDDDG). If you do not click, the player does not load. The consent applies to the current page view only: reload the page to return to the preview.

More information: Google Privacy Policy.
