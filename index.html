How every episode is made

Every claim can be traced to its source.

  1. 01

    Research first

    Every episode starts with the research, not with the script.

  2. 02

    Cited like a paper

    Every figure is numbered [1], [2] and listed in a full reference list.

  3. 03

    Redrawn, never pasted

    Charts are rebuilt from the source data, not copied from a report.

  4. 04

    Nothing invented

    Illustrative examples are labelled as illustrative. Everything else has a source.
